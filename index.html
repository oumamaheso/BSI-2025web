About Us

Our company, BSI South Africa (BSI SA), specializes in delivering financial services and training solutions to individuals and businesses. We offer portfolio management, financial planning, and advisory services,as well as training programs in financial services, debt recovery, and business development. Our Company is divided into three pillars, BSI College, BSI Accounting and training solutions and BSI Finex.
